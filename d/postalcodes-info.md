PostalCodes.info is an open-access global postal code and administrative hierarchy reference covering **249 ISO-3166 countries and territories** with verified WGS84 latitude/longitude centroids, UPU S42 address validation patterns, downloadable country CSV sample extracts, and a Free Tier JSON REST API.

It structures national postal systems (such as US 5-digit ZIP codes, Dutch 6-character postcodes, German 5-digit PLZ, UK alphanumeric postcodes, Canadian FSAs, and Spanish 5-digit INE/Correos codes) into a unified, normalized schema (`country_iso2, postal_code, place_name, admin1_name, admin2_name, latitude, longitude`) designed for GIS analysis, logistics routing, checkout address validation, and cross-border catchment calculations.

License: CC0 1.0 Public Domain for downloadable 50-row country sample CSVs and MIT License for open-source client examples.
